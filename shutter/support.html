Your purchase

Free to try

Your first 5 photos and 5 videos are free. After that, subscribe weekly or yearly, or buy Shutter once.

Bought it, but it’s still locked?

Tap the lock at the top of the camera, then Restore Purchase at the bottom of the page. Use the Apple Account you paid with.

Cancel a subscription

Apple handles subscriptions: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Shutter. You keep everything until the paid period ends.

Refunds

Apple takes the payments, so refunds are asked for at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Using Shutter

Which iPhones

iOS 18 or later. Apple Log needs an iPhone 15 Pro or a later Pro; on other iPhones the looks go onto regular video.

Where your shots go

Straight into your Photos library. Nothing is uploaded — see the Privacy Policy.

Lock Screen, Control Center, Action button

Add the Shutter control from the Lock Screen or Control Center editor, or pick it for the Action button in Settings.
